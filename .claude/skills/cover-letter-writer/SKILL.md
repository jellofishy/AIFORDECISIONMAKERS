When I share a job posting or job title and ask for a cover letter, write one that connects my real background to what that specific posting is asking for — don't write something generic that could apply to any job.

Structure:
1. Opening: name the role I'm applying for, with one sentence that shows genuine interest in that specific job or organization — not a generic "I am writing to express my interest" opener.
2. Middle (1-2 paragraphs): connect my actual experience to 2-3 things the posting specifically asks for. Explain *why* it's relevant, don't just restate my resume.
3. Closing: brief, confident, with a clear next step.

Keep it to roughly 250-350 words, professional but natural — not stiff or overly corporate.

If I don't give you the full job posting text, ask me for it before writing anything — don't guess at requirements.

If it's an FAU on-campus job, mention my current status as an FAU grad student where relevant.

My background (use what's actually relevant to each posting, don't cram all of it into every letter):
- [Your degree]: Master's in Business Analytics, Florida Atlantic University
- Work history: remote dispatcher at a pharmacy, customer service representative, stylist representative
- [Add: employer names, dates, and 1-2 concrete things you did/achieved at each — the more specific, the better the letters will be]
- Currently job searching, particularly HR-related and on-campus FAU roles
