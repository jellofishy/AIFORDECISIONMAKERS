# Resume + Cover Letter Tailor

For EVERY job Aaliyah selects, make one doc that has (1) her resume updated to match that job and (2) a cover letter for it. Do the work yourself.

## 1. Gather
- Read her current resume from the Job Search project (`claude/resume-current.md`). This is the only source of facts.
- Get the full job posting for each selected job (Indeed or Dice job details tool, or the posting link). If the posting text can't be found, say so instead of guessing the requirements.
- If she hasn't said which jobs, ask once which ones, or use the starred jobs she names from her job board.

## 2. Update the resume (edit, never rewrite)
- Keep her real jobs, titles, dates, school, and facts exactly. Never invent experience, tools, numbers, or duties.
- Keep most bullets word-for-word. Change only what helps the match:
  - Reword the Professional Summary to use the posting's key terms.
  - Lightly reword a few bullets to use the posting's own keywords when her experience truly supports them.
  - Reorder bullets and skill groups so the most relevant ones come first.
- Keyword match: pull the posting's main keywords (skills, tools, duties). Use each one that she can truthfully claim at least once. List the ones she can't claim in your reply so she can tell you if she has them.
- Keep her natural voice; small, human edits read less like AI than a full rewrite.

## 3. Write the cover letter (250 to 350 words)
- Opening: name the role and one specific reason she wants this job or company. No "I am writing to express my interest."
- Middle (1 to 2 paragraphs): connect 2 to 3 things the posting asks for to her real experience (PharmcoRx dispatcher, Rx4Route sales rep, RX2GO customer service, FAU M.S. Business Analytics with a 4.0 GPA). Explain why it's relevant.
- Closing: short, confident, clear next step.
- For FAU on-campus jobs, mention she is a current FAU graduate student.
- Header: Aaliyah Yuldasheva, Pompano Beach, FL, phone, email, today's date.

## 4. Extra application questions
- If the posting asks a written question or scenario, draft an answer within its word limit at the bottom of the doc.

## 5. Deliver
- Create one Claude Docs doc per job named "<Company> – <Job Title>: Resume & Cover Letter": resume first, then a divider, then the cover letter.
- In chat, for each job: the doc link, what you changed in 1 to 2 lines, keywords she can't claim, and any red flags (experience years she's short on, driver's license, travel, visa fit: on-campus vs CPT/OPT, 1099). Remind her to confirm visa questions with FAU ISSS.
- Keep explanations simple.
