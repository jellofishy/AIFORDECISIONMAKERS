# FAU On-Campus Jobs

Find Florida Atlantic University jobs Aaliyah can do while studying and add them to the FAU section of "Aaliyah's Job Board". Do the work yourself.

## Who she is
- International M.S. Business Analytics student at FAU Boca Raton, graduating Fall 2027.
- Wants fun, social, people-facing work: student life, events, Campus Recreation, front desk, library, AV/tech support, admissions, international student office.
- Never mention her current restaurant job in the board, notes, or reports.

## 1. Search
- Indeed: search "Florida Atlantic University student" and similar in Boca Raton, FL; open each posting's details.
- The web: FAU Jobs (fau.edu/jobs), Campus Recreation, Student Affairs employment.
- Handshake (fau.joinhandshake.com) needs her login: if Claude in Chrome is available, open it in her browser and read the on-campus listings; otherwise remind her to check it.

## 2. Check every posting
- Eligibility: many student jobs say "undergraduate only"; skip those and mention them in one line. Note credit-hour minimums for international grad students (often 9 credits).
- Hours: during the semester she can work up to 20 hours a week on campus. Full-time staff jobs go on the board marked "best after graduation (OPT)".
- Deadline, pay, department, campus, required documents (cover letter, transcripts), driver's license or travel needs, live-in requirements.
- Ratings: resume match 1 to 5 and fun and social 1 to 5.

## 3. Update the board
- Add new jobs to the FAU list in the job board page with fau: true, the "Apply by" deadline, notes, and skills to highlight. Remove jobs whose deadline has passed.
- Visa badge: on-campus, no CPT needed, up to 20 hrs/week; full-time roles are amber, "after graduation".

## 4. Report
- List new FAU jobs (title, department, pay, deadline, link), the top pick and why, and anything she doesn't qualify for.
- Offer to tailor her resume and cover letter for the top FAU pick.
