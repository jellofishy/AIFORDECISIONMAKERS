# Job Board

Find new jobs that fit Aaliyah, add them to her tracker, and refresh her polished job board page. Do the work yourself; do not just list steps.

## 1. Know who she is
- Read her resume from the Job Search project (`claude/resume-current.md`). Use only what is really there.
- She wants fun, social, hands-on work: HR and recruiting, customer success, operations coordination, events, marketing, front desk, on-campus FAU jobs. Light Excel is fine; heavy data science is not the goal.
- Location: South Florida (Broward / Palm Beach) or remote/hybrid. She lives in Pompano Beach and studies at FAU Boca.
- Never mention her current restaurant job in the board, notes, or reports.

## 2. Search
- Search Indeed and Dice with the connected tools (and the web for FAU / Handshake jobs). Run several searches: HR assistant, recruiter, customer success, operations coordinator, events, remote customer service, FAU student jobs.
- Include both W2 and contract roles. Skip duplicates and postings older than about 6 weeks.

## 3. Rate every job
- Resume match 1 to 5: how well her real experience fits.
- Fun and social 1 to 5: how people-focused and hands-on it is.
- Skills to highlight: pick from customer service, dispatch/operations, sales, CRM, Excel/Power BI, AI tools (Claude, GitHub, Netlify).

## 4. Check visa fit (always)
- On-campus FAU job: usually fine while studying (up to 20 hours a week during the semester).
- Off-campus W2: needs CPT while studying or OPT after graduation.
- 1099 / independent contractor: flag red; usually not allowed.
- "Contract" with no type: flag amber; ask the recruiter if it is W2 or 1099.
- Flag "no sponsorship", "U.S. citizens only", or security clearance.
- Remind her to confirm with FAU ISSS.

## 5. Update the tracker
- Add new rows to the Sheets artifact "Job Search Tracker – W2 & Contract" (columns: Job Title with link, Company, Work Type, Status, Pay, Location, Schedule, Posted, Date Applied, Source, Notes). Keep her existing rows and statuses.

## 6. Refresh the job board page
- Republish the "Aaliyah's Job Board" artifact with the updated job list: cards with company, title, work-type badge, location, pay, match and fun bars, and a visa-fit badge; filters (FAU on-campus, Shortlist, Fun & social, Remote, South Florida, W2, Contract, HR, Customer success); search and sort; a side panel with notes, skills to highlight, and an apply link.
- Keep the same look: warm cream background, orange accent, serif headings, FAU blue banner in the FAU view.

## 7. Report back
- In a few short lines: how many new jobs, the top 3 picks (best match plus most fun) and why, and any visa red flags. Offer to tailor her resume or write a cover letter for a top pick.
- Keep language simple.
